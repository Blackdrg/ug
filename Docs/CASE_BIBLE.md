# CASE BIBLE: THE 13TH FILE
**Subject:** The Blackwood Research Facility Disappearance (November 14, 2009)  
**Setting:** Blackwood Facility Sub-Level 3 (Cryo-Genomics & Neuro-Mapping)  

---

## 1. The Core Mystery Premise

In 2009, an unauthorized blackout and catastrophic explosion sealed Blackwood Sub-Level 3. The official government inquest cited an "arc flash electrical fire in Primary Substation B." Thirteen researchers and staff were declared dead or missing. No remains were returned to families.

Seventeen years later (2026), Investigator **Julian Ward** receives an unmarked titanium drive containing a photograph of the facility gate, a manifest listing twelve researchers, and a ciphered note:

> *"Twelve people vanished. One person was never there. Find the thirteenth file before they find you."*

## 3. The Contradiction Triad (Chapter 2 & 3 Core Clues)

- **Contradiction 1: The Missing Power Cut**
  - *Claim*: Marcus Vance testifies: *"The main grid blew out at midnight sharp (00:00). All monitors went black."*
  - *Counter-Evidence*: Security Camera Node #04 log shows feed severed at **23:42**.
  - *Corroboration*: Substation B Telemetry logs uninterrupted emergency backup power between 23:30 and 01:15.
  - *Deduction*: Marcus Vance manually cut the surveillance 18 minutes prior to stage the fire.

- **Contradiction 2: The 13th Employee Roster**
  - *Claim*: Official Department of Energy inquest lists "Dr. Paul Vance - Deputy Director, joined March 2008."
  - *Counter-Evidence*: Personnel Payroll ledger 2008-2009 has zero payments to any Paul Vance.
  - *Counter-Evidence*: Marcus Vance's personal military service record lists him as an only child.
  - *Deduction*: "Dr. Paul Vance" is a dummy identity inserted into the archive.

- **Contradiction 3: The Fatal Burns vs Frostbite**
  - *Claim*: Death certificate states victim in Lab 03 perished from intense thermal burns.
  - *Counter-Evidence*: Autopsy polaroid reveals severe liquid nitrogen cryogenic freeze-fracture and cyanosis.
  - *Deduction*: The "fire" was a cover story; victims were locked into the sub-zero neural isolation chamber.

## 4. Endings & Player Deductions

1. **Ending A — The Official Patsy (Incorrect Theory)**:
   Player accuses Marcus Vance of sole murder and arson. Marcus is arrested posthumously, but the secret neural overwrite project remains hidden in the dark.
2. **Ending B — The Corporate Whistleblower (Partial Truth)**:
   Player exposes Dr. Alistair Finch's illegal experiments and uncovers Elena Cruz's audio logs. Finch's estate is raided, but the identity of the 13th person remains an enigma.
3. **Ending C — The 13th File Unlocked (True Detective Ending)**:
   Player links the forged identity of Dr. Paul Vance, the cryogenic lock, and the Subject Zero neural telemetry. Player discovers: **You were Subject 13**, given false memory implants 17 years ago after surviving the cryogenic breach.
4. **Secret Ending D — The Janus Inheritance**:
   Player accesses Finch's terminal using the master override code discovered through UV light on the archive seal (`JANUS-13-AWAKE`), uploading the uncorrupted neural archives to the global grid.
